# Security policy

If you find a security issue in an Accel Comply website or service, please tell us. We read every report.

## Scope

accelcomply.com and its subdomains.

Out of scope: third-party services we use (hosting, mail, CRM), denial of service, social engineering, spam and physical attacks.

## How to report

Email security@accelcomply.com with a description of the issue, the steps to reproduce it and the impact you expect. Please do not include personal data you came across beyond what is needed to show the issue.

## What happens next

We acknowledge your report within 5 business days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved. If you want credit, we will name you once the fix is in place.

We treat good-faith research that stays within this policy as welcome, not hostile. Please give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it publicly.

There is no bug bounty.
